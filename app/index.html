Missable watch list

One trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else on the list is confirmed safe to leave for free-roam after the story — the items players worry about most are listed below so you can stop worrying about them.

⚠ Possibly missable — do this in Chapter 1

Positive Mental Attitude bronze

Meditate at every spot across Leonida — 18 spots at 10 locations. The first meditation is the trap: after the Chapter 1 mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept the meditation. It is gone for good after Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether it is required — PowerPyx did it and earned the trophy, so do it to be safe.

Source: PowerPyx, GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap, 18 Nov 2026 · the only missable PowerPyx flags.

✓ Confirmed safe — nothing to rush

  • All 12 story trophies — automatic on finishing chapters and missions; none can be missed. PowerPyx, 18 Nov 2026
  • Every character questline (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) — quest givers stay available after the story. PowerPyx, 18 Nov 2026
  • Strangers & Freaks (8 strands) — not part of the 100% checklist and all doable in free-roam. PowerPyx, 18 Nov 2026
  • 100% checklist items — vehicles, animals, challenges, stunts, robberies, vaults: all available after the credits. PowerPyx, 18 Nov 2026
  • Bank vaults & armored trucks — unlock in Chapter 4 and stay open in free-roam. PowerPyx, 18 Nov 2026

No mission replay exists, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter anyway (15 manual save slots, 1 autosave) — and never turn on a cheat, because cheats disable trophies and saving. PowerPyx, 18 Nov 2026
