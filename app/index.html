Road to the platinum: Vice Got a Price!

Around 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies and no mission medals. The story is 5 chapters and takes roughly 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist is the long grind after it.

Stage 1 — Play the story

60–80 hours · Chapters 1–5

Finish all 5 chapters and the story trophies unlock as you go — none of them can be missed. Scan vehicles and study animals whenever you see them; progress already counts toward 100%.

⚠ Only missable step: Positive Mental Attitude

During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept the meditation. It is gone for good after Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether it is required — do it to be safe.

Stage 2 — 100% completion

~100 hours · after the story

The checklist unlocks under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% of true completion. You need any 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied and 300 stunts; only robberies, underwater finds, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests need everything.

Nothing here is missable. This stage earns A Long Road (gold) and most of the activity trophies on the way.

Stage 3 — Strangers & cleanup

~10 hours · free-roam

Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, the remaining questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), the money trophies and any collectibles left — meditations, card sets, WAiNK scans, garages and vehicle registrations.

Every quest giver stays available after the story, so nothing here can lock you out.

Stage 4 — Platinum

Vice Got a Price!

When the other 54 trophies are earned, the platinum pops automatically. Before you celebrate: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay — and never turn on a cheat, because cheats disable trophies and saving.

15 manual save slots, 1 autosave slot. Use them.
